This repository contains the R code accompanying our paper on the Fermi–Dirac distribution. We provide functions for computing the distribution's characteristics using both numerical and MCMC approaches, and we compare some of these characteristics with their theoretical counterparts. For further details, please refer to the paper.

For readers interested in related particle models, we also recommend our previous work:  
*M. Shams, M. A. Mirzaie, Statistical Inference and Simulation for the Maxwell‐Boltzmann Distribution, Advanced Theory and Simulations, 8(9), (2025), 2500148: 1-40, doi: https://doi.org/10.1002/adts.202500148.*

# Abstract of the Fermi-Dirac paper
The Fermi–Dirac distribution has long played a central role in the modeling of physical systems, particularly in quantum statistical mechanics. In this study, we investigate particle models governed by the Fermi–Dirac distribution and analyze their statistical properties, including moments, moment generating function, maximum likelihood estimation, Fisher information, entropy, and Kullback–Leibler divergence. While closed-form expressions are available for some quantities in the FD2 case (such as Fisher information and entropy), the remaining quantities are evaluated numerically. Based on the maximum likelihood estimator and Fisher information, Wald type confidence intervals are constructed and hypothesis testing procedures are discussed. To facilitate simulation, random samples are generated using the integral probability transformation method, which is preferred due to its computational efficiency and accuracy. Furthermore, for two independent Fermi–Dirac distributed random variables, we examine the distribution of functions derived from them along with their moments. The practical relevance of the proposed framework is demonstrated through an application to real data. The paper concludes with several directions for future research.
